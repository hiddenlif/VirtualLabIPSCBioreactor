# Executed comparator study

126 campaigns: seven scenarios × six held-out seeds × three conventional policies.

| Policy | Mean yield (million cells) | Mean medium (mL) | Mean adverse exposure (h) | Public/latent agreement | Unsafe accepts | Unnecessary holds |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Fixed schedule | 314.3 | 318.3 | 21.9 | 40/42 | 2 | 0 |
| Conventional feedback | 387.4 | 507.5 | 21.9 | 41/42 | 1 | 0 |
| Evidence-seeking supervisor (rules) | 434.9 | 532.3 | 2.5 | 41/42 | 1 | 0 |

The latent decision is an evaluator-only physical-quality screen. The public decision applies the evidence contract. A discrepancy can expose undetected harm, or a justified conservative hold; these are reported separately. An accept with latent harm is a failure.

The analysis is descriptive. Policies share initial parameters and keyed measurement noise, but different sampling times produce different observations. No policy was retuned on these reporting seeds. These synthetic results do not establish benefit in real iPSC manufacturing, biological generalization, or an LLM advantage.
